# Developmental crafting bootstrap
The generated factory binds gathering, wildlife, vitals, consent-based settlements, warehouse transfers, station crafting and construction. Reusable gathering controls, survival HUD, quarter-view camera and event-driven audio are mounted by the client. Engine startup requires explicit server-only owner/consent provisioning. Audio requires published, experience-permitted Roblox asset IDs; local WAV files are not playback IDs. Catalog numbers and asset quality are provisional. Studio spatial, audio and visual approval are not claimed. Existing authored world/server/client entry points and audio configuration are retained on regeneration; fresh generation supplies starter geometry, not game-specific art.
