# Sky Whale Egg Keepers - Store Listing

## Title and hook

- English title: **Sky Whale Egg Keepers**
- One-line hook: **Raise the Sky Whale Egg Keepers roster, merge up a rarity, and siege the rival vault every 10 minutes.**
- Concept source: `sky whale egg keepers defend floating islands`
- Locales: `en-us` (`game.yml locales`).

## Description (3 paragraphs)

**Paragraph 1 - hatch and merge.** Buy an egg, hatch it in seconds, and let the creature earn Coins at the base; spend those Coins on bigger eggs, merge 3-of-a-kind up a rarity, and join the team siege every 10 minutes to raid the rival vault.

**Paragraph 2 - team siege.** Two teams each defend a shared vault. Every siege period a window opens for a fixed duration and squads move up to max_take eggs from the enemy vault. Personal bases are never a raid target.

**Paragraph 3 - fair monetization.** Everything in the store is deterministic: paid Robux items say exactly what they grant, and egg odds never change. Random hatching runs on free Coins only, so no Robux is ever spent on a random roll.
